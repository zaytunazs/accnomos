Privacy policy

Accnomos · Zaytuna Business Services Ltd · last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

Accnomos keeps your books in your own Microsoft OneDrive. We do not run a user database and we do not store your receipts, invoices, ledger or VAT figures on our servers. The AI that reads your documents runs either on a machine you control or through an API key you provide.

What the app accesses

  • Microsoft account (sign-in): your name and e-mail, used only to show who is signed in.
  • OneDrive app folder: the app reads and writes files inside its own Accnomos folder (inbox, ledger events, Books.xlsx, VAT returns). It does not read the rest of your drive.
  • Camera / photos (Android): only when you choose to capture or pick a receipt; the image is uploaded to your OneDrive inbox.
  • Your AI connection: either the address of a model running on your own hardware (e.g. Muse Glimmer on Ollama), or a Meta Model API key you paste in. The key is stored on your device and sent only to our stateless runtime over HTTPS to make the request on your behalf; it is never logged or stored server-side.
  • HMRC (sandbox during the hackathon): if you connect HMRC, the OAuth tokens stay in your browser session. Our relay holds only the HMRC application secret and forwards requests; it stores nothing about you. HMRC requires fraud-prevention headers (device, screen size, time zone, IP address) which the relay forwards with each request.

What we keep

Nothing personal. The runtime and relay are stateless: requests are processed in memory and discarded. Standard web-server access logs (IP address, path, time) are kept for up to 30 days for security.

Third parties

  • Microsoft (sign-in and OneDrive) — under your Microsoft account terms.
  • Meta Model API — only if you provide your own key; subject to Meta's terms for your key.
  • Baseten — only if you use your own key and the server owner has switched on the Muse Spark Open verifier; the document image is then sent there for an independent second reading. In local mode documents are never sent to Baseten.
  • HM Revenue & Customs — only if you connect HMRC.

We do not sell data and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Deleting your data

Delete the Accnomos folder in your OneDrive and sign out of the app. Because we hold no copy, that removes everything.
