Help centre

Step-by-step guides for the web and the Android app, and how to reach us when a guide doesn't help.

Getting started

  • Sign up and connect your storageTry the fictional demo with no sign-in, or sign in with Microsoft or Google so your books live in a folder in your own OneDrive or Google Drive.Web · Android · 4 steps

Receipts and review

  • Upload receipts, or drop a whole folder at onceAdd photos, PDFs and bank CSVs one at a time, or drop a zip or a whole folder; everything lands in 00 Inbox and is read for you.Web · Android · 4 steps
  • Review and approveCheck each reading against the document, then approve, edit or reject. Nothing reaches your books until you approve it.Web · Android · 4 steps

Bank

  • Connect a bank feed and reconcileBring bank lines in from a feed or a statement CSV, then confirm the matches. Matching never posts anything on its own.Web · Android · 4 steps

Invoices and PayPal

  • Send an invoice with a PayPal pay linkConnect PayPal once, then add a Pay online link to any invoice. When the customer pays, the payment waits on Invoices for you to approve.Web · Android · 4 steps
  • Sync PayPalOne tap reads your PayPal history and proposes each transaction for your books: invoice payments, other sales, purchases and fees. Nothing posts until you approve it.Web · 3 steps
  • Respond to a PayPal disputeWhen a customer disputes a payment made through one of your invoice links, Accnomos builds the evidence from your books; you preview it and send it to PayPal.Web · 4 steps
  • Pay supplier bills with PayPalSave a supplier's PayPal email, then pay their approved bills through PayPal on the due date, one at a time or with Autopilot inside limits you set.Web · 4 steps

VAT and month-end

  • Do a VAT return and send it to HMRCPick the quarter, check the nine boxes worked out from your approved entries, then connect to HMRC and submit, or make a bridging file.Web · Android · 5 steps
  • Close the monthOne run checks the month: documents waiting, bank lines, bills, invoices, payroll and VAT. You clear what blocks it, then approve the close.Web · 3 steps

Assistant and apps

  • Use the assistant by voice or chatAsk about your books or get something done in plain words, typed or spoken. Figures come from your books; anything that changes them comes back as a card to approve.Web · Android · 5 steps
  • Use the Android appThe same books on your phone: Home, Review with the camera, VAT, Ask and the Talk button. Settings and connecting accounts are done on the web.Android · 4 steps

Help and support

  • Get help and raise a support ticketAsk the support assistant first; if it can't solve it, raise a ticket. You choose what is attached, and replies appear on the Support page.Web · Android · 4 steps
  • PayPal problems: where to get helpWhat Accnomos can sort out for you, and what only PayPal can: account limits, holds, refunds and disputes are handled in PayPal's own Resolution Centre and help pages.Web · 3 steps
