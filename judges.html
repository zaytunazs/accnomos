Try the demo

For judges

Try Accnomos in one click

This sandbox is specially for testing. It opens six months of books for a fictional company, Bramblewood Joinery Ltd (every document in it is labelled as fictional sample data), with no Microsoft sign-in and no two-step verification. Each visitor gets their own copy, kept in memory on our server and reset after 2 hours, so nothing you change reaches anyone else.

What to try, in this order

  1. 1

    Review a receipt

    Six documents are waiting. Open one: what the AI read, the checks code ran on it, and the journal it would post. Two need a look (an invoice in euros and a malformed VAT number).

  2. 2

    Approve

    Approve a receipt: nothing is posted until you do. The entry lands in the ledger and Books.xlsx, and can be undone with a reversing entry.

  3. 3

    VAT return

    The 9 boxes, worked out by code from approved entries, with the entries behind each box. Connect to HMRC and submit to see the whole flow; in the demo it ends on a receipt labelled “Demo — submission simulated”.

  4. 4

    Ask

    Ask “How much VAT do I owe this quarter?” or “Send a £100 invoice to Hartwell Property Maintenance”. The figures come from code; the AI only explains them, and an action waits for your Approve.

  5. 5

    Reports

    Profit and loss, aged debtors and creditors, the VAT summary and the trend, over six months of books.

  6. 6

    Contacts

    Customers and suppliers built from the ledger: who owes what, which suppliers aren't VAT-registered, and Import from Outlook (a demo address book).

  7. 7

    Bank

    Two imported statements reconciled against the books, bank rules coding the regular lines, and what is still to match.

What is real here, and what is simulated

  • Real: the app, the checks, the ledger and VAT arithmetic, and the AI (the model this server is set up with). Scanning the documents still in 00 Inbox sends them to that AI. Which Meta model does what.
  • Pre-read: the six documents waiting in Review were read when the sample books were made, from the fields printed on them, so you can start without waiting for a model.
  • Simulated: email is never sent (you'll see “Sent (demo — not actually sent)”), HMRC is never contacted, and the Companies House and VAT-number look-ups are switched off.
  • Not stored: nothing about you. There is no Microsoft account behind the demo, and its books are gone after 2 hours idle or when you press Reset demo.

Your own books instead

Signing in with Microsoft sets up real books in a folder in your own OneDrive: Accnomos keeps no copy, and HMRC submissions go to HMRC's sandbox. You can still load the same sample books there with one button. Connect your OneDrive.
