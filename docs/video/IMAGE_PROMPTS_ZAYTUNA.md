# Image prompts: Zaytuna palette (Muse Image)

The six cards the judges' video (3:00 script) uses, re-drawn in the Zaytuna brand palette. Same format as
`IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`; made with `muse-image-1.0` through `tools/video/muse_images.py` (prompts file and output folder
pointed here), saved to `docs/video/cards-zaytuna/<id>.png` with a JSON sidecar each. As before, no text is drawn into
the pictures: `assemble.sh` adds every word on top.

Credit wherever this art appears: **Title art: Muse Image (Meta).**

## style

size: none
use: prepended to every prompt below

Flat, clean vector illustration with soft geometric shapes and gentle depth, calm, warm and trustworthy, for a UK
small-business bookkeeping app. Palette only: rich brown #5A2D00, deep brown #2A1A0D, warm off-white #F8F6F3, muted
gold #C9A45C and olive green #8A9A3B. No text, no letters, no numbers, no words, no logos or brand marks of any real
company, no real people and no faces, no screenshots of real software, no flags, no currency symbols. Leave a wide calm
area of plain colour where a title will be added later.

## judges-title

size: 1920x1080
use: opening provenance card of the judges' video (shot 1) and its fallback card

A quiet workbench seen from slightly above at dusk: a closed notebook, a paper receipt and a phone lying face down on
a deep brown desk, a small sprig of olive leaves beside them, a warm gold desk lamp glow in the lower right corner. The
left two thirds stay almost empty deep brown for the title.

## section-impact

size: 1920x1080
use: background for Impact cards (shots 2–3) when a shot is a card

A British high street of small independent shops drawn as simple flat shapes: a joiner's workshop, a café and a
corner shop with plain blank signs, a small olive tree in a pot by one door, morning light, warm off-white sky. The top
half is open sky for a title.

## section-meta-ai

size: 1920x1080
use: background for the measured-numbers card and other Meta AI tooling cards

Abstract: paper receipts drifting from the left turn into tidy rows of a ledger grid on the right, a soft gold glow
where they pass through a simple lens shape. Deep brown background, off-white paper shapes, thin olive lines. The
centre stays calm and dark so a table of figures can sit on it.

## section-depth

size: 1920x1080
use: background for Technical depth cards

An isometric stack of neat paper ledger sheets on the left, each sealed with a small gold wax-seal shape, beside a
simple folder shape on the right; clean lines, rich brown, olive and off-white on deep brown. Middle third left empty.

## section-demo

size: 1920x1080
use: background for Demo quality cards and the end card

A single paper receipt following a dotted gold path across the frame: from a phone on the left, through an inbox
tray, to a ledger book and a sealed envelope on the right, a few olive leaves along the path. Deep brown background,
off-white, gold and olive shapes, wide empty band across the middle.

## thumb-judges

size: 1280x720
use: thumbnail of the judges' video

Bold, simple composition: one large off-white paper receipt tilted slightly, a rich brown check-mark shape stamped on
it, a small olive sprig and gold corner accent, deep brown background, the right half empty for a title.
