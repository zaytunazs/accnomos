Each clip is self-contained at 0:00–0:35 (35 s). Voice-over runs at about 2.5
words per second; every line below fits its shot. All data shown is the demo
sandbox's sample company, labelled SAMPLE — FICTIONAL COMPANY. HMRC contact is
sandbox only.
