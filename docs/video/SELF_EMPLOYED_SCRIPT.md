# Accnomos for the self-employed: video script (draft 1, 11 Nov 2026)

One video, about 3 min 30 s. British English female voice-over (Kokoro `bf_emma`). Screen recordings of the
test-mode demo with a fictional sole trader ("Sam Fletcher, joiner"); every name, receipt and figure is fictional and
the video says so in the first card.

| # | Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:12 | Title card, then the Home page of the sole-trader demo | If you're self-employed in the UK, your books shouldn't eat your evenings. This is Accnomos. Everything shown uses a fictional business. |
| 2 | 0:12–0:50 | A pile of 30+ mixed files (receipt photos, PDF invoices, a utility bill, a bank statement CSV) dragged into Review in one go; **Scan 00 Inbox now**; cards appear, sorted and categorised; the counter ticks | Start by dumping everything you've got. Receipt photos, PDF invoices, bills, bank statements: drop them all in at once, or save them to the inbox folder from your phone. Accnomos reads every one, works out what it is, pulls out the supplier, date, VAT and total, and puts each into the right category. |
| 3 | 0:50–1:15 | One card opened: the original beside the reading, the "Why" line, checks passed; **Approve**; a trusted supplier ticked "Always do this" | Nothing goes into your books until you say so. Each card shows the original, what was read, and why it chose that category. Approve, edit or reject. Tick "always do this" for suppliers you trust, and next time they're done for you, with an undo. |
| 4 | 1:15–1:45 | Bank page: a live bank feed syncing, or a statement uploaded; lines matched to receipts | Connect your bank, or upload a statement. Payments are matched to the receipts you've approved, so you can see straight away what's missing a receipt. |
| 5 | 1:45–2:10 | Ask: "What profit have I made this quarter?", then "What did I spend on fuel?", answers with sources | Ask questions in plain English. How much profit you've made, what you spent on fuel, which customers still owe you. Every answer links back to the entries behind it. |
| 6 | 2:10–2:45 | Income Tax page: the year's figures in HMRC's boxes; the quarterly update figures; **File it yourself** export | When it's time for tax, your figures are already laid out the way HMRC asks for them: box by box for Self Assessment, and in HMRC's categories for the quarterly updates under Making Tax Digital. Export them and file them yourself, or hand them to your accountant. |
| 7 | 2:45–3:05 | VAT page (for a VAT-registered sole trader): the nine boxes and the transactions behind each | If you're VAT registered, your VAT return is worked out from your approved entries, with every box traceable to the documents behind it. |
| 8 | 3:05–3:22 | Reports › exports for Xero, QuickBooks and FreeAgent; Invite your accountant | Working with an accountant? Invite them in, or export everything in a format Xero, QuickBooks or FreeAgent can import. |
| 9 | 3:22–3:35 | Files: the Accnomos folder in the user's own OneDrive / Google Drive; end card accnomos.com | And your records stay yours, in your own OneDrive or Google Drive. Accnomos. Your books, done while you work. |

## Truth checks before filming

- Scene 2: the multi-file drop and inbox scan exist today. If zip / whole-folder drop is merged first, show a zip too;
  otherwise don't say "zip".
- Scene 6: say "file them yourself" unless HMRC production credentials for Income Tax are in place (not possible for
  new products in 2026-27).
- Scene 7: say "file it with your own HMRC account" only once VAT production credentials are approved and live;
  until then the line stays as written (no filing claim).
- Scene 4: live bank feeds are TrueLayer's sandbox in the demo; the line doesn't name a bank or claim live coverage.
