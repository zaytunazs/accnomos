# Demo video script

The video submitted is **v3** (4 Nov 2026, 2:55.9, AI voice-over): script and shot list in
[`docs/video/SCRIPT_V3.md`](../video/SCRIPT_V3.md), scene times and edit notes in
[`docs/video/V3_EDIT.md`](../video/V3_EDIT.md), the judges' end slide in [`docs/video/JUDGES_VIDEO.md`](../video/JUDGES_VIDEO.md),
tools in `tools/video/v3/`.

It follows the organisers' rules of 4 Nov: three minutes or less, opening with one line on what existed before the
hackathon and what was built during it. Every claim in the voice-over and on screen is one `docs/HONESTY.md` allows; the
figures on the judges' slide are from `docs/submission/META_MODELS.md` and `evals/runs/*/summary.json`. HMRC filing in the
video goes to HMRC's sandbox, said on the lower third, the end card and the judges' slide.

Earlier scripts: v2 (`docs/video/SCRIPT_V2.md`, the owner's narrative, 3 Nov) and the 28 Oct phone-first script, which
is in git history (`git log -- docs/submission/VIDEO_SCRIPT.md`). The 28 Oct script's "status on main" table is out of
date: the features it listed as not on `main` (profiles, the models panel, the no-sign-in demo) were merged and are in
the v3 video.
