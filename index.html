How it works

Four steps for every document. Below, one receipt goes through them on real screens from the app.

  1. Step 1

    Send it in

    Photograph a receipt in the Android app, upload it on the web, or let receipts arrive from your email inbox. You can also just say what you need.

  2. Step 2

    The AI proposes

    An AI model reads the document and suggests supplier, category, VAT and totals. Code checks the arithmetic.

  3. Step 3

    You approve

    Each proposal waits in Review. Approve, edit or reject. Nothing posts on its own.

  4. Step 4

    VAT and records, ready

    Approved entries build your ledger, your VAT boxes and a spreadsheet copy of your books.

The rest of the books, in the same place

Receipts, the bank, PayPal, pay runs and month-end, all from the same approved entries.

  • Receipts from email and photos

    Snap a receipt on your phone or let supplier emails be picked up from your inbox. The AI reads each one into a proposal you check.

    Example: A £60 plus VAT invoice from a fictional supplier, Brightfix Electrical Ltd, arrives by email and waits in Review with supplier, date and totals filled in.

  • Bank lines, matched and coded

    Import a bank statement as a CSV file and see each line matched to entries already in your books. Confirm the matches and deal with the rest one by one.

    Example: A fictional £240 payment to Brightfix Electrical Ltd matches the bill for the same amount dated four days earlier.

  • PayPal, in the same approve-first books

    Put a PayPal pay link on your invoices, then Sync PayPal: each sale, purchase and fee is proposed with its journal, you choose the VAT on a sale with no invoice, and the PayPal balance is tied out against your books. Tried with PayPal's sandbox; nothing posts until you approve it.

    Example: A fictional customer pays a £372.00 invoice through its PayPal link; the payment and PayPal's £11.09 fee wait on Invoices until you tap Approve.

  • Supplier bills paid through PayPal

    Approve a bill now and it is paid from your PayPal on its due date, only if nothing about it changed. Upcoming payments go in your calendar, a dispute's evidence is built from your books, and one switch stops every automatic payment.

    Example: A fictional £51.90 bill from Novex Office Supplies Ltd, approved today, is paid on 12 November, the day it falls due.

  • Pay runs, estimated in pence

    Enter hours and rates to get a pay-run estimate with tax and National Insurance shown line by line. These are estimates for planning, not payroll filings.

    Example: A fictional employee, Tom Ellis, on 20 hours at £13.50 an hour gets a gross of £270 with deductions listed beside it.

  • Month-end checks, listed plainly

    Run a month-end check that lists missing statements, uncategorised costs, possible duplicates and anything dated into a closed month. Work through the list and re-close when done.

    Example: Fictional March books for Bramblewood Joinery Ltd flag two unmatched bank lines and one receipt still sitting in the inbox.

Making Tax Digital, for VAT and Income Tax

Figures for HMRC come from code, never from a model.

VAT boxes, worked out by code

Your nine VAT boxes are calculated by code from approved entries, not typed by hand. Download them as a bridging file to file through your bridging software, or practise the HMRC flow on the sandbox.

Example: A fictional quarter for Harthill Bakery Ltd shows £1,240 in Box 1 and £310 in Box 4.

Income Tax for the self-employed

If you are self-employed, quarterly Income Tax figures are worked out from your approved entries, with the ledger lines behind each box. The HMRC connection here is sandbox only.

Example: A fictional sole trader, Emma Hughes, sees £18,400 of turnover for the period to 5 July with each line traceable.

Voice and chat that wait

Ask a question in plain words, typed or spoken, and get an answer with figures checked against your books. Short instructions become cards you approve first.

Example: “Andy did 2 hours on the Hendon job” becomes a timesheet card for the Hendon job, and nothing is recorded until you tap Approve.

Spoken questions are turned into text by a speech-to-text model; it is used live, and measured on synthetic recordings only.

For accountants

Have your books reviewed by an accountant: your own, or ours. Either way they open the same files you see: the ledger, Books.xlsx, reports and the audit trail.

  • Invite your own accountant

    Enter their email and choose view only or view and edit. Your Accnomos folder in OneDrive is shared with their Microsoft account, and they open your books in the Accountant portal. Remove access at any time and the sharing stops at once.

  • Or opt in to a review by Accnomos accountants

    Where it is offered, you can opt in to have our own accounting team review your books. It is a separate, explicit choice: you read what they can see before you agree, and Stop reviews removes the sharing at once. They never submit anything to HMRC for you and never move money.

Both are in Settings › Your accountant. An accountant can leave a query on a transaction or document, which you answer there; with edit access, every change they make is recorded in your audit trail under their name, and anything they question can be undone with a reversing entry, never a delete. Accountants open their clients' books in the Accountant portal.

Your books live in OneDrive

Your ledger, documents and spreadsheets are files in your own OneDrive folder. Accnomos keeps no database of your books.

Example: Disconnect and the fictional folder for Bramblewood Joinery Ltd, with its ledger events and Books.xlsx, is still sitting in your OneDrive.

AI proposes, you approve

Every figure that reaches your books has passed code checks in whole pence, and a second reading checks the first on key documents. There is no database of your books on our side: the files live in your OneDrive. Every approve, reject, match, payment and undo is written to an audit trail you can open.

Questions

Something else? Ask the chat, which answers from the team's notes.

How much does it cost?+

Every account starts with a 3-month free trial, no card needed. After that, Starter is £10, Comprehensive £20 and Ultimate £50 a month ex VAT (2 months free if you pay yearly). The demo with sample books costs nothing to try. See the plans.

Where are my books kept?+

In your own OneDrive, as files you can open. We keep no database of your books.

Does it file to HMRC?+

The HMRC connection in the product today is sandbox only, for VAT and for self-employed Income Tax. It is there to practise the full flow, not to file a live return. HMRC has closed production access for new Income Tax quarterly-update products for the 2026-27 tax year, so live Income Tax filing from Accnomos cannot start before the 2027-28 year; live VAT filing needs a separate HMRC production application. Filing to HMRC from Accnomos is planned. Until then, File it yourself gives you the VAT return as a bridging file to upload to HMRC-recognised bridging software, the Income Tax quarterly figures for MTD software, and your Self Assessment self-employment pages box by box.

Can I use it outside the UK?+

Yes. Choose your country when you set up: you say whether you charge VAT, GST or sales tax and at what rate, and your books are kept in your own currency. Bookkeeping, Review, invoices, reports and CSV exports work the same; HMRC filing, payroll and Making Tax Digital are UK only and stay hidden.

Which banks are covered?+

You import bank statements as CSV files. Barclays, Starling, Tide and Monzo style layouts are read, along with HSBC and Lloyds style paid-in and paid-out columns.

Can I use it on my phone?+

There is an Android app with capture, Review, Ask, VAT and reports. Everyday admin such as bank uploads and detailed settings stays on the web.

Can I switch from Xero or QuickBooks?+

Export your lists and statements from your current software and bring them across as files. Your opening position is entered once, and new documents flow in from there.

How do I start the free trial?+

Sign in with Microsoft: the 3-month trial starts at once and needs no card. See what each plan includes.

Does my accountant need an account?+

Only a Microsoft account. In Settings, Your accountant, you invite them by email and choose view only or view and edit: your Accnomos folder in OneDrive is shared with them, and they open your books in the Accountant portal. Remove access there at any time.

Can an accountant review my books for me?+

Where it is offered, you can opt in, in Settings, Your accountant, to have Accnomos accountants, our own accounting team, check your books. It is separate from inviting your own accountant: you read what they can see before you agree, and Stop reviews removes the sharing at once. They never submit anything to HMRC for you and never move money.

What can the AI get wrong?+

It can misread a total, a date or a VAT number, most often on crumpled photos or faint print. That is why every reading is checked by code and waits for your approval before it posts.

How do I stop?+

Export your files first if you wish, then disconnect. Your books are already files in your OneDrive, so nothing is locked in.

Is my data used to train models?+

We do not use your documents to train models: they are read to keep your books, and nothing else. When your server uses an API key, the document goes to that model provider under its own terms.
