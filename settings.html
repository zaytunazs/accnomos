Second reading (verifier)

Every extraction that passes the code checks is read again by a second model with a different prompt that never sees the first answer. Code compares six fields: when all agree, the Review card says so; any disagreement sends the proposal to Needs a look showing both values. If the second reading cannot run, the proposal waits for you — that is the safety design, not a pass.

Online payments

Put a “Pay online” link on your invoices, paid into your own Stripe or PayPal account. Accnomos keeps only the account id in your OneDrive, never a password or key. A payment that comes in is shown for you to approve; the provider's fee goes in as its own expense.

VAT periods

On your VAT certificate or in your HMRC online account. Used for your deadlines.

What is stored where

  • Your books live in your OneDrive, in the Accnomos app folder (personal accounts) or /Accnomos (work accounts): inbox, receipts, sales, bank statements, VAT returns, reports, Books.xlsx and _System (the ledger events, audit trail and working data).
  • This browser keeps your sign-in, your AI choice and key, VAT settings and display choices in its own localStorage (HMRC tokens live only in the tab). Disconnect removes them from this browser.
  • The runtime keeps nothing. It reads and writes your OneDrive with the token each request carries, answers, and discards everything. There is no Accnomos user database.

Leave Accnomos

Disconnect clears this browser's sign-in and settings; your files stay in your OneDrive. Disconnect Accnomos

To delete the books, delete the Accnomos folder in your OneDrive. Because the runtime keeps no copy, that removes everything.

To revoke the app's Microsoft permission, remove Accnomos at account.live.com/myapps.

No server-side delete is needed, because nothing is stored server-side.
