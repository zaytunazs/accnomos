Privacy policy

Accnomos · Zaytuna Business Services Ltd · last updated 29 October 2026

The short version

Accnomos keeps your books in your own Microsoft OneDrive. We do not run a user database and we do not store your receipts, invoices, ledger or VAT figures on our servers. The AI that reads your documents runs either on a machine you control or through an API key you provide. The one exception is the 3 months free list, which holds only what you type into it if you choose to sign up.

What the app accesses

  • Microsoft account (sign-in): your name and e-mail, used only to show who is signed in.
  • OneDrive app folder: the app reads and writes files inside its own Accnomos folder (inbox, ledger events, Books.xlsx, VAT returns). It does not read the rest of your drive.
  • Sending invoices (Outlook, optional): the first time you send an invoice or a payment reminder, the app asks Microsoft for permission to send mail as you (Mail.Send). It sends only the email you saw and confirmed, to the address you confirmed, with the invoice PDF attached; a copy is in your Sent Items and the PDF in your Accnomos folder. Reminder wording is drafted by your own AI connection from the invoice number, amount and dates; your bank details are not sent to the AI.
  • Camera / photos (Android): only when you choose to capture or pick a receipt; the image is uploaded to your OneDrive inbox.
  • Your AI connection: either the address of a model running on your own hardware (e.g. Muse Glimmer), or a Meta Model API key you paste in. The key is stored on your device and sent only to our stateless runtime over HTTPS to make the request on your behalf; it is never logged or stored server-side.
  • HMRC (sandbox during the hackathon): if you connect HMRC, the OAuth tokens stay in your browser session. Our relay holds only the HMRC application secret and forwards requests; it stores nothing about you. HMRC requires fraud-prevention headers (device, screen size, time zone, IP address) which the relay forwards with each request.

What we keep

Nothing from your books. The runtime and relay are stateless: requests are processed in memory and discarded. Standard web-server access logs (IP address, path, time) are kept for up to 30 days for security. If you sign up for 3 months free, we keep what you typed into that form, as described next.

The 3 months free list

You can leave your email on our website to be set up with 3 months free of Accnomos. Signing up is optional and has nothing to do with using the app.

  • Who: Zaytuna Business Services Ltd, United Kingdom, is the controller of the list. Contact admin@zaytunauk.co.uk.
  • What we collect: your email address; your name and how you heard about us, if you give them; what the books would be for (limited company, sole trader, personal, or accountant/bookkeeper); that you ticked the consent box and its exact wording; the page you signed up on; and the date and time. We do not store your IP address with it.
  • Why: to email you to set up your 3 months free, and to know how many people of each kind have signed up. Nothing else: no newsletter, no other products, and we never sell or share the list.
  • Lawful basis: your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given by ticking the box, which is never pre-ticked. Our email also follows the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.
  • Where it is kept: in one file on a server we run ourselves, readable only by the service operator. Nobody else receives it. If we use an email-sending service to email you, we will name it here first; it would act only on our instructions.
  • How long: we delete the list by the end of November 2027, except for people who have become customers. Unsubscribing deletes your entry straight away.
  • Unsubscribe or erase: reply to our email, or email admin@zaytunauk.co.uk from the address you signed up with. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and we will delete your entry and confirm. You can also ask for a copy of what we hold, or for it to be corrected.
  • Complaints: if you are unhappy with how we handle your data, tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

No decisions are made about you automatically, and you do not have to sign up to use Accnomos.

Third parties

  • Microsoft (sign-in and OneDrive) — under your Microsoft account terms.
  • Meta Model API — only if you provide your own key; subject to Meta's terms for your key.
  • A hosted inference provider chosen by the server owner — only if you use your own key and the server owner has switched on the Muse Spark Open verifier; the document image is then sent there for an independent second reading. In local mode documents are never sent there.
  • HM Revenue & Customs — only if you connect HMRC.

We do not sell data and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs. The 3 months free list is not shared with anyone.

Your data: export and leaving

Export. Settings → Your data → Export your data saves everything as one zip in 07 Reports/Exports in your OneDrive (accnomos-export-<date>.zip): ledger events, proposals, contacts, invoices with an index of the invoice PDFs, payments, bank lines, rules, the audit trail and the saved VAT returns — plus a README explaining each file and one CSV per main table (ledger lines, contacts, invoices, payments) so it opens in Excel. A second export the same day is kept beside the first, never overwritten.

Where things live. Your books live in your OneDrive, in the Accnomos app folder. This browser keeps your sign-in, AI choice and settings in its own localStorage (HMRC tokens live only in the tab). The runtime keeps nothing: it works on your OneDrive with the token each request carries and discards everything. There is no Accnomos user database.

Leaving. Disconnect clears this browser's sign-in and settings; your files stay in your OneDrive. To delete the books, delete the Accnomos folder in your OneDrive and remove Accnomos at account.live.com/myapps. No server-side delete is needed, because nothing from your books is stored server-side (the 3 months free list is separate) .

Deleting your data

Delete the Accnomos folder in your OneDrive and sign out of the app. Because we hold no copy, that removes everything. There is no server-side copy of your books to delete.
