Privacy policy

Accnomos · Zaytuna Business Services Ltd · last updated 11 November 2026

The short version

Accnomos keeps your books in your own Microsoft OneDrive, or in your own Google Drive if you sign in with Google. We keep a small account registry (who you are, your plan and how much you have used it) but we do not store your receipts, invoices, ledger, bank lines or VAT figures on our servers. Unless you choose otherwise, the AI that reads your documents runs on our own server. Only if it cannot answer does the document go to our backup AI provider, Anthropic (in the United States), under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train models on your content. Since 11 November 2026 our server sends no documents to the hosted model's provider. You can instead run the AI on a machine you control or use your own API key (see where your documents go). The one exception is the waiting list, which holds only what you type into it if you choose to sign up.

What the app accesses

  • Microsoft account (sign-in): your name and e-mail, used only to show who is signed in.
  • OneDrive app folder: the app reads and writes files inside its own Accnomos folder (inbox, ledger events, Books.xlsx, VAT returns). It does not read the rest of your drive.
  • Sending invoices (Outlook, optional): the first time you send an invoice or a payment reminder, the app asks Microsoft for permission to send mail as you (Mail.Send). It sends only the email you saw and confirmed, to the address you confirmed, with the invoice PDF attached; a copy is in your Sent Items and the PDF in your Accnomos folder. Reminder wording is drafted by your own AI connection from the invoice number, amount and dates; your bank details are not sent to the AI.
  • Google account (sign-in, if you choose Google): your name and e-mail, used only to show who is signed in. Your books go in an Accnomos folder in your Google Drive (My Drive), and the app can see only the files it created there, never the rest of your Drive.
  • Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Contacts (Google sign-in): Google asks for these at sign-in, all at once (see the table below). Gmail is read to find bills and receipts and the people who email you; Accnomos writes a draft or sends an email only when you have seen it and confirmed it. Calendar events are read to plan your day, and Accnomos writes only the events you approve. Contacts are read so you can tick which to add to your books; nothing is written back.
  • Camera / photos (Android): only when you choose to capture or pick a receipt; the image is uploaded to your books inbox (OneDrive or Google Drive).
  • Your AI connection (optional): by default none: our server's own AI chain is used (see below). If you choose it, either the address of a model running on your own hardware, or an API key for the hosted model that you paste in. The key is stored on your device and sent only to our stateless runtime over HTTPS to make the request on your behalf; it is never logged or stored server-side.
  • HMRC (sandbox; filing to HMRC from Accnomos is planned): if you connect HMRC, the OAuth tokens stay in your browser session. Our relay holds only the HMRC application secret and forwards requests; it stores nothing about you. HMRC requires fraud-prevention headers (device, screen size, time zone, IP address) which the relay forwards with each request.

Where your documents go for AI reading

The AI reads a document, or a question you ask, only when you ask it to. Where that request is processed depends on your AI choice in Settings.

  • Default (you have made no AI choice): the AI on our own server reads it. We do not keep the request.
  • Backup, only when our server cannot answer: if our own server is down, too busy or cannot give a usable reading, the same document or question is sent to Anthropic (Anthropic PBC, United States) through its API, under Anthropic's Commercial Terms, which say it may not train models on the content it receives, and its Data Processing Addendum. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days unless it must keep them longer to enforce its usage policy or by law (Anthropic's retention note); we do not have a zero-retention arrangement with it.
  • The hosted model's provider: since 11 November 2026 our server sends no documents, questions or recordings to the hosted model's provider. Before that date our live server used the hosted model's API in a mode in which the provider may use the content to improve its models; the demo used only fictional sample data.
  • Local mode (your choice): the document goes to the model address you give, on hardware you control, and to nobody else. Spoken questions are transcribed on our own server.
  • Your own key (your choice): the document goes to the hosted model's API under your key, in standard mode, subject to that provider's terms for your key. It does not use the server owner's key.
  • Optional extra provider: a hosted inference provider may be added to the server's chain as an optional extra provider; it is not used today and this page will be updated if that changes.

Spoken questions are converted to text on our own server and the audio is not kept.

Your account, plan and billing

To run plans and billing we keep an account registry in a Supabase database in a UK or EU region. It holds account details only: your email address, how you signed in (Microsoft or Google) and a stable id from that sign-in, your plan, billing status and trial dates, any promo codes you redeemed, monthly usage counts (documents read, questions asked, invoices created) and notes our support team adds when helping you. It never holds your books, documents, receipts, bank lines, financial records, or tokens for other services.

Problem reports. If you use Report a problem, we keep your message, the page you were on, the app version, your browser or phone type and a screenshot only if you choose to attach one, until the report is closed and for 12 months after.

Usage analytics and error reports

First-party analytics. The app records which pages and features are used, with counts and durations, against your account id, so we can see what works. It goes to our own registry through our own server: no third-party tracker, no cookies, no fingerprinting, and never amounts, supplier names, document text, emails or file names. Events are kept for 13 months. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy (“Do not collect usage analytics”) and nothing more is recorded.

Error reports (Sentry, when enabled). When switched on, errors in the web app, our server and the Android app are sent to Sentry, in its EU data region, with personal data turned off and amounts, emails, VAT numbers and tokens removed before sending. Where it is not switched on, nothing is sent.

What we keep

Nothing from your books, only the account registry above. The runtime and relay are stateless: requests are processed in memory and discarded. Standard web-server access logs (IP address, path, time) are kept for up to 30 days for security. If you join the waiting list, we keep what you typed into that form, as described next.

The waiting list

You can leave your email on our website to join the Accnomos waiting list. Signing up is optional and has nothing to do with using the app.

  • Who: Zaytuna Business Services Ltd, United Kingdom, is the controller of the list. Contact zohaibsharif@gmail.com.
  • What we collect: your email address; your name and how you heard about us, if you give them; what the books would be for (limited company, sole trader, personal, or accountant/bookkeeper); that you ticked the consent box and its exact wording; the page you signed up on; and the date and time. We do not store your IP address with it.
  • Why: to email you when your place on the waiting list comes up, and to know how many people of each kind have signed up. Nothing else: no newsletter, no other products, and we never sell or share the list.
  • Lawful basis: your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given by ticking the box, which is never pre-ticked. Our email also follows the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.
  • Where it is kept: in one file on a server we run ourselves, readable only by the service operator. Nobody else receives it. If we use an email-sending service to email you, we will name it here first; it would act only on our instructions.
  • How long: we delete the list by the end of November 2027, except for people who have become customers. Unsubscribing deletes your entry straight away.
  • Unsubscribe or erase: reply to our email, or email zohaibsharif@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and we will delete your entry and confirm. You can also ask for a copy of what we hold, or for it to be corrected.
  • Complaints: if you are unhappy with how we handle your data, tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

No decisions are made about you automatically, and you do not have to sign up to use Accnomos.

Third parties

  • Supabase — hosts our account registry (plan, billing status, usage counts, analytics events, problem reports) in a UK or EU region; never your books or financial records.
  • Sentry (EU region) — receives scrubbed error reports, only where error reporting is enabled.
  • Microsoft (sign-in and OneDrive) — under your Microsoft account terms.
  • Google (sign-in, Google Drive, and Gmail, Calendar and Contacts) — only if you sign in with Google, under your Google account terms.
  • Our own server — runs the AI that reads your documents and questions and transcribes spoken questions; nothing is kept.
  • Anthropic (Anthropic PBC, United States) — our backup AI provider and a processor: it receives a document or question only when our own server cannot answer, under its Commercial Terms (no training on your content) and Data Processing Addendum. Not used in local mode or with your own key.
  • The hosted model's provider — only with your own API key, if you provide one, under that provider's terms for your key. Our server sends it nothing of yours.
  • Our web host (IONOS, the server that terminates HTTPS for accnomos.com and api.accnomos.com) — carries your requests, including document content, over an encrypted tunnel to our own server; it is a processor for that transport only.
  • TrueLayer — only if you connect a bank for bank feeds; it receives your bank sign-in choice and consent, and returns account and transaction data, which we write to your OneDrive.
  • PayPal — only if you connect it for payment links on invoices; it receives the invoice amount, reference and description needed to create the link, and tells us when it is paid.
  • A hosted inference provider chosen by the server owner — only if you use your own key and the server owner has switched on a hosted second-reading model; the document image is then sent there for an independent second reading. It is off today. In local mode documents are never sent there.
  • HM Revenue & Customs — only if you connect HMRC.

We do not sell data, we use no advertising, and we have no third-party analytics scripts or cookies. The waiting list is not shared with anyone.

Your data: export and leaving

Export. Settings → Your data → Export your data saves everything as one zip in 07 Reports/Exports in your OneDrive (accnomos-export-<date>.zip): ledger events, proposals, contacts, invoices with an index of the invoice PDFs, payments, bank lines, rules, the audit trail and the saved VAT returns — plus a README explaining each file and one CSV per main table (ledger lines, contacts, invoices, payments) so it opens in Excel. A second export the same day is kept beside the first, never overwritten.

Where things live. Your books live in your OneDrive, in the Accnomos app folder. This browser keeps your sign-in, AI choice and settings in its own localStorage (HMRC tokens live only in the tab). The runtime keeps nothing: it works on your OneDrive with the token each request carries and discards everything. There is no copy of your books on our side: only the account registry above.

Leaving. Disconnect clears this browser's sign-in and settings; your files stay in your OneDrive. To delete the books, delete the Accnomos folder in your OneDrive and remove Accnomos at account.live.com/myapps (signed in with Google: delete the Accnomos folder in your Google Drive and remove Accnomos at myaccount.google.com/permissions). No server-side delete of your books is needed, because nothing from them is stored server-side; to delete your account registry entry, email us (the waiting list is separate) .

Deleting your data

Delete the Accnomos folder in your OneDrive (or Google Drive) and sign out of the app. Because we hold no copy, that removes all of your books. To delete your account registry entry as well (plan, usage counts, reports), email us from your sign-in address; cancel any paid plan first in Settings → Plan & billing.
