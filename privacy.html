Privacy policy

Accnomos · Zaytuna Business Services Ltd · last updated 29 October 2026

The short version

Accnomos keeps your books in your own Microsoft OneDrive. We do not run a user database and we do not store your receipts, invoices, ledger or VAT figures on our servers. Unless you choose otherwise, the AI that reads your documents is the one on our server: Meta's Model API, then a fallback model on our own server. You can instead run it on a machine you control or use your own API key (see where your documents go). The one exception is the waiting list, which holds only what you type into it if you choose to sign up.

What the app accesses

  • Microsoft account (sign-in): your name and e-mail, used only to show who is signed in.
  • OneDrive app folder: the app reads and writes files inside its own Accnomos folder (inbox, ledger events, Books.xlsx, VAT returns). It does not read the rest of your drive.
  • Sending invoices (Outlook, optional): the first time you send an invoice or a payment reminder, the app asks Microsoft for permission to send mail as you (Mail.Send). It sends only the email you saw and confirmed, to the address you confirmed, with the invoice PDF attached; a copy is in your Sent Items and the PDF in your Accnomos folder. Reminder wording is drafted by your own AI connection from the invoice number, amount and dates; your bank details are not sent to the AI.
  • Camera / photos (Android): only when you choose to capture or pick a receipt; the image is uploaded to your OneDrive inbox.
  • Your AI connection (optional): by default none: our server's own AI chain is used (see below). If you choose it, either the address of a model running on your own hardware (e.g. Muse Glimmer), or a Meta Model API key you paste in. The key is stored on your device and sent only to our stateless runtime over HTTPS to make the request on your behalf; it is never logged or stored server-side.
  • HMRC (sandbox during the hackathon): if you connect HMRC, the OAuth tokens stay in your browser session. Our relay holds only the HMRC application secret and forwards requests; it stores nothing about you. HMRC requires fraud-prevention headers (device, screen size, time zone, IP address) which the relay forwards with each request.

Where your documents go for AI reading

The AI reads a document, or a question you ask, only when you ask it to. Where that request is processed depends on your AI choice in Settings.

  • Default (you have made no AI choice): our live server's chain. The request goes first to Meta's Model API using the server owner's key; if that cannot answer, to a fallback model running on our own server. We do not keep the request; Meta's own retention is described in its terms and, in Contributor mode, above.
  • Contributor mode: our live server uses Muse Spark 1.3 in Contributor mode for the demo and testing. In Contributor mode Meta may use the content sent to it to improve its models. The demo uses only fictional sample data; anyone trying it with their own books is served the same way, so please test with documents you are happy to share. Before real customers use it, the server switches to standard mode.
  • Local mode (your choice): the document goes to the model address you give, on hardware you control, and to nobody else. The one exception is spoken questions: the audio is transcribed with Meta's Model API using the server's key, in every mode.
  • Your own key (your choice): the document goes to Meta's Model API under your key, in standard mode, subject to Meta's terms for that key. It does not use the server owner's key.
  • Optional extra provider: a hosted inference provider may be added to the server's chain as an optional extra provider; it is not used today and this page will be updated if that changes.

Spoken questions are converted to text and the audio is not kept.

What we keep

Nothing from your books. The runtime and relay are stateless: requests are processed in memory and discarded. Standard web-server access logs (IP address, path, time) are kept for up to 30 days for security. If you join the waiting list, we keep what you typed into that form, as described next.

The waiting list

You can leave your email on our website to join the Accnomos waiting list. Signing up is optional and has nothing to do with using the app.

  • Who: Zaytuna Business Services Ltd, United Kingdom, is the controller of the list. Contact zohaibsharif@gmail.com.
  • What we collect: your email address; your name and how you heard about us, if you give them; what the books would be for (limited company, sole trader, personal, or accountant/bookkeeper); that you ticked the consent box and its exact wording; the page you signed up on; and the date and time. We do not store your IP address with it.
  • Why: to email you when your place on the waiting list comes up, and to know how many people of each kind have signed up. Nothing else: no newsletter, no other products, and we never sell or share the list.
  • Lawful basis: your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given by ticking the box, which is never pre-ticked. Our email also follows the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.
  • Where it is kept: in one file on a server we run ourselves, readable only by the service operator. Nobody else receives it. If we use an email-sending service to email you, we will name it here first; it would act only on our instructions.
  • How long: we delete the list by the end of November 2027, except for people who have become customers. Unsubscribing deletes your entry straight away.
  • Unsubscribe or erase: reply to our email, or email zohaibsharif@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and we will delete your entry and confirm. You can also ask for a copy of what we hold, or for it to be corrected.
  • Complaints: if you are unhappy with how we handle your data, tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

No decisions are made about you automatically, and you do not have to sign up to use Accnomos.

Third parties

  • Microsoft (sign-in and OneDrive) — under your Microsoft account terms.
  • Meta Model API — the default for AI reading on our live server (server owner's key, Contributor mode for the demo and testing: Meta may use the content sent to it to improve its models), and with your own key if you provide one, under Meta's terms for that key. It receives the document or question being read. Not used in local mode.
  • Our own server — runs the fallback model if Meta's Model API cannot answer, and receives the same content; it is not kept.
  • Our web host (IONOS, the server that terminates HTTPS for accnomos.com and api.accnomos.com) — carries your requests, including document content, over an encrypted tunnel to our own server; it is a processor for that transport only.
  • TrueLayer — only if you connect a bank for bank feeds; it receives your bank sign-in choice and consent, and returns account and transaction data, which we write to your OneDrive.
  • Stripe and PayPal — only if you connect either for payment links on invoices; they receive the invoice amount, reference and description needed to create the link, and tell us when it is paid.
  • A hosted inference provider chosen by the server owner — only if you use your own key and the server owner has switched on the Muse Spark Open verifier; the document image is then sent there for an independent second reading. In local mode documents are never sent there.
  • HM Revenue & Customs — only if you connect HMRC.

We do not sell data and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs. The waiting list is not shared with anyone.

Your data: export and leaving

Export. Settings → Your data → Export your data saves everything as one zip in 07 Reports/Exports in your OneDrive (accnomos-export-<date>.zip): ledger events, proposals, contacts, invoices with an index of the invoice PDFs, payments, bank lines, rules, the audit trail and the saved VAT returns — plus a README explaining each file and one CSV per main table (ledger lines, contacts, invoices, payments) so it opens in Excel. A second export the same day is kept beside the first, never overwritten.

Where things live. Your books live in your OneDrive, in the Accnomos app folder. This browser keeps your sign-in, AI choice and settings in its own localStorage (HMRC tokens live only in the tab). The runtime keeps nothing: it works on your OneDrive with the token each request carries and discards everything. There is no Accnomos user database.

Leaving. Disconnect clears this browser's sign-in and settings; your files stay in your OneDrive. To delete the books, delete the Accnomos folder in your OneDrive and remove Accnomos at account.live.com/myapps. No server-side delete is needed, because nothing from your books is stored server-side (the waiting list is separate) .

Deleting your data

Delete the Accnomos folder in your OneDrive and sign out of the app. Because we hold no copy, that removes everything. There is no server-side copy of your books to delete.
