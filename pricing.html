Good to know

Bring your own AI. Use your own API key or a local model on any plan. AI-read documents and Ask then become fair use, with no monthly cap, and the price does not change.

Top-ups. Need more documents in a busy month? 100 extra AI-read documents cost £3, valid for 12 months, on any plan.

Nothing is ever locked away. At a limit, new reading pauses with a friendly note; approving, editing and exporting what is already in your books always works. If a trial ends without a card, your books become read-only and are never deleted.

Your books stay yours. They live in your own OneDrive; we keep only your account, plan and usage counts.

For comparison, comparable plans from well-known UK accounting software cost about £14 to £56 a month ex VAT, and tools that only read documents charge roughly £0.21 to £0.31 a document. Accnomos Comprehensive is £20 a month and includes 300 (checked 9 November 2026).
