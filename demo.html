Demo

Your demo of Accnomos

Six months of books for a fictional small business, Bramblewood Joinery Ltd, with nothing to set up and no sign-in. Every document in it is labelled as fictional sample data. You get your own copy, kept in memory on our server and reset after 2 hours, so nothing you change reaches anyone else, and nothing about you is kept.

Also: the personal demo

Accnomos also keeps a household's own money. The personal demo opens a fictional family's bank lines sorted into budgets, their bills and subscriptions, and goals such as saving for school fees or spending less on coffee, each tracked against their books with suggestions.

What to try

In this order if you like, or jump to whatever you do most.

  1. 1

    Open a receipt that is waiting for you

    Six documents are in Review. Open one and see what the AI read from it, the checks that were run on it, and the entry it would put in your books. Two of them need a look: an invoice in euros, and a VAT number that is not quite right.

  2. 2

    Approve it, or change it first

    Nothing goes into your books until you say so. Approve a receipt and the entry lands in the ledger and in Books.xlsx; if you change your mind, undo it and a reversing entry is posted. Add a receipt of your own with the Add a receipt button and watch it read.

  3. 3

    Ask, by typing or by talking

    Ask “How much VAT do I owe this quarter?” or “Who owes me the most?”, or press Talk to Accnomos and say it. The figures come from your books, worked out by code; the AI only explains them. Ask it to send a £100 invoice to Hartwell Property Maintenance and it drafts one that waits for your Approve.

  4. 4

    Raise an invoice

    Invoices with VAT, due dates and who has paid. Send one: in the demo nothing is actually emailed, and the page says so (“Sent (demo — not actually sent)”).

  5. 5

    Match the bank

    Two imported statements matched against the books, bank rules that code the regular lines for you, and what is still to match. Connect the test bank to see a live feed come in, labelled as the sandbox it is.

  6. 6

    File a VAT return to HMRC’s test service

    The nine boxes, worked out by code from the entries you approved, with the entries behind each box. Connect to HMRC’s sandbox with a test user made for this demo, submit, and get HMRC’s own receipt back. No real tax is filed.

  7. 7

    Set a goal and see how it is going

    Three business goals sized from these books: this month’s sales, spending in the busiest purchase category, and a VAT pot. Each is tracked against the ledger, with a suggestion when one slips.

  8. 8

    Reports and contacts

    Profit and loss, aged debtors and creditors, the VAT summary and the trend over six months. Contacts are built from the ledger: who owes what, and which suppliers are not VAT-registered.

What is real in the demo, and what is not

  • Real: the app, the checks, the ledger and VAT arithmetic, and the AI that reads your documents. Scanning anything still in 00 Inbox, or a receipt you add, sends it to that AI. Which Meta model does what.
  • Already read: the six documents waiting in Review were read when the sample books were made, so you can start without waiting for a model.
  • HMRC sandbox: the VAT return goes to HMRC's own test service, with a test user made for your demo, HMRC's sign-in page, its periods and its receipt. No real tax is filed; filing for real needs HMRC's approval. If the sandbox can't be reached, the card says so and simulates the submission instead.
  • Simulated: email is never sent (you'll see “Sent (demo — not actually sent)”), the bank feed is a test bank, and the Companies House and VAT-number look-ups are switched off.
  • Not kept: anything about you. There is no account behind the demo, and its books are gone after 2 hours idle or when you press Reset demo.

Ready for your own books?

Sign in with Microsoft and Accnomos sets up real books in a folder in your own OneDrive: it keeps no copy, and HMRC submissions go to HMRC's sandbox. You can load these same sample books there with one button, to try things on before your own paperwork arrives. Connect your OneDrive.
