Respond to a PayPal dispute

When a customer disputes a payment made through one of your invoice links, Accnomos builds the evidence from your books; you preview it and send it to PayPal.

Web · updated 11 November 2026

  1. 1Step 1: Find the dispute

    Open Invoices. PayPal disputes lists each dispute on a payment Accnomos knows about, with the invoice it matched, the reason and when PayPal needs your answer.

  2. 2Step 2: Build the evidence

    Press Build evidence. Code gathers the invoice, when and to whom it was emailed, the payment and your reminders into one PDF, and drafts a short factual response. Every figure and date in the draft is checked against your books.

  3. 3Step 3: Preview, then send

    Read Response for PayPal (preview) and the PDF, then press Send to PayPal. Nothing reaches PayPal before you press it.

  4. 4Step 4: If the dispute is lost

    When PayPal refunds the whole payment, Accnomos offers the reversing entry so the invoice is open again; it is posted only if you say yes.

If this goes wrong

Not a payment Accnomos knows about
Answer this one in PayPal's Resolution Centre: there is no record of the payment in your books.
Could not check PayPal disputes
Check PayPal is still connected in Settings › Online payments. The Support page lists the latest PayPal problem when you raise a ticket.

Still stuck?

Ask the support assistant or raise a ticket (signed in).
