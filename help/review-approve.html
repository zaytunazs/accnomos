Review and approve

Check each reading against the document, then approve, edit or reject. Nothing reaches your books until you approve it.

Web and Android app · updated 11 November 2026

  1. 1Step 1: Open a card

    In Review, open the document from its card and compare it with the reading: supplier, VAT number, date, net, VAT and total.

  2. 2Step 2: Read the checks

    Read the Why line, the second reading and the checks by code, and the proposed entry. Then press Approve (post to books), Edit or Reject.

  3. 3Step 3: Move through the queue

    The next card opens. Keys work too: J/K move, A approves, E edits, R rejects and U undoes the last approval.

  4. 4Step 4: Let Autopilot handle trusted suppliers

    Tick Always do this for … on a supplier you trust and Autopilot approves their next documents that pass every check. Undo any of them from Approved by Autopilot: Undo adds a reversing entry, it never deletes.

If this goes wrong

The two readings disagree
The card waits in Needs a look with both values: pick the one the document supports.
The image is unreadable
Reject the card and photograph the receipt again.
Autopilot did not approve a bill
A bill much larger than usual waits in Review even with a rule: Autopilot only posts up to twice the usual.
