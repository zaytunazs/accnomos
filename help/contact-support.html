Get help and raise a support ticket

Ask the support assistant first; if it can't solve it, raise a ticket. You choose what is attached, and replies appear on the Support page.

Web and Android app · updated 11 November 2026

  1. 1Step 1: Open Support

    Open Support (/support), signed in. Ask the support assistant answers "how do I…" questions from these articles, with a link to the one it used.

  2. 2Step 2: Raise a ticket

    If the answer doesn't help, or something is broken, the assistant offers a ticket card with a summary, a category and the steps. Check it, then press Create ticket. You can also press New ticket and fill in the form yourself.

  3. 3Step 3: Choose what to attach

    Tick Attach recent errors to send the last error messages the app saw (no figures). Tick Include PayPal details to send whether PayPal is connected, the last sync and the last PayPal problem. Financial details are only sent if you tick Include financial details.

  4. 4Step 4: Follow the thread

    Your tickets are listed on Support with their status: Open, Waiting for you or Solved. Open one to read replies and answer.

If this goes wrong

Support says it is not set up on this server
Email the address at the bottom of the Help page instead.
You can't sign in at all
Use Report a problem on the Help page or email us: a ticket needs a sign-in.

Still stuck?

Ask the support assistant or raise a ticket (signed in).
