Use the assistant by voice or chat

Ask about your books or get something done in plain words, typed or spoken. Figures come from your books; anything that changes them comes back as a card to approve.

Web and Android app · updated 11 November 2026

  1. 1Step 1: Open the assistant

    Press Talk to Accnomos (bottom right of every page). Type a question, pick a suggestion, tap the mic for one spoken question, or press Hands-free and just talk.

  2. 2Step 2: Read the answer

    Figures sit beside the answer with the files they came from, and a button takes you to the page.

  3. 3Step 3: Approve cards

    When it proposes something it shows a card. Hands-free reads it back and asks "Shall I go ahead?"; a clear yes approves notes, to-dos and events. Cards that send or move money always need a tap on Approve.

  4. 4Step 4: Find a payment

    Ask "there was a PayPal payment last month, around £100 for stationery: how much exactly?" or "did that client pay the arrears? check PayPal". The AI reads your words into a search; code searches your books, Sync PayPal and PayPal itself, and gives the exact amount, date and who it was with, and says whether it is in your books yet. It ends by saying what it searched.

  5. 5Step 5: Ask how to do something

    Ask "how do I…" and the assistant answers from these help articles, with a link to the article. If that doesn't solve it, it offers a support ticket card.

If this goes wrong

The mic is refused
Allow the microphone for accnomos.com in your browser and try again.
The answer shows no figures
Name the period, for example "this quarter" or "in September".

Still stuck?

Ask the support assistant or raise a ticket (signed in).
