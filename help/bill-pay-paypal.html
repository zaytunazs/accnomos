Pay supplier bills with PayPal

Save a supplier's PayPal email, then pay their approved bills through PayPal on the due date, one at a time or with Autopilot inside limits you set.

Web · updated 11 November 2026

  1. 1Step 1: Open Purchases

    Open Purchases (/purchases) and find Pay bills with PayPal. Due today lists approved bills whose due date has come.

  2. 2Step 2: Save the supplier's PayPal email

    Under Suppliers' PayPal emails, add the email the supplier gave you and press Save PayPal email. Payees are never taken from a document.

  3. 3Step 3: Pay a bill

    Press Pay on a bill. PayPal processes it; once PayPal says it succeeded, the payment and PayPal's fee are posted against the bill.

  4. 4Step 4: Optional: Autopilot for bills

    Turn on Pay automatically with Most per bill (£) and Most per month (£). A bill whose payment details changed is held for you, and Stopped switches everything off at once.

If this goes wrong

Payment details changed on a bill
Check with the supplier by phone before paying, then tick I checked with the supplier.
Waiting for the supplier to claim it
The supplier has no PayPal account for that email yet; nothing is posted until they claim it.
A bill was not paid on its due date
Payments run when Accnomos is open on or after the due date; open Purchases.
