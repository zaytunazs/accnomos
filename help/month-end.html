Close the month

One run checks the month: documents waiting, bank lines, bills, invoices, payroll and VAT. You clear what blocks it, then approve the close.

Web · updated 11 November 2026

  1. 1Step 1: Open Month-end

    Open Month-end (/month-end) and pick the month. The checks cover documents waiting in 00 Inbox or Review, statement coverage and unmatched bank lines, bills due, overdue invoices, the pay run and, if the month ends a VAT period, the nine boxes.

  2. 2Step 2: Clear what blocks the close

    Blocking items must be cleared first; each has a button to the page that clears it. Matches and reminders that code suggests come as cards to approve.

  3. 3Step 3: Approve the close

    Read The month's figures before and after, then approve the close card. The P&L and balance sheet are saved as a draft in 07 Reports. Closing posts nothing.

If this goes wrong

This card has expired
Run the close again: cards last 15 minutes.
The close is refused
Something in the month changed after the card was made; run it again.
