Connect your OneDriveSee the demo booksHow it works

  • Your books live in your OneDrive

    Receipts, ledger, Books.xlsx and VAT returns sit in your own OneDrive folder. Accnomos runs no user database and keeps no copy.

  • AI proposes, you approve, code calculates

    The AI reads each receipt and proposes an entry; nothing is posted until you approve it. The ledger and the 9-box VAT return are worked out by deterministic code, never by a model.

  • AI you control

    Run Meta's open-weight Muse Glimmer on your own machine, or bring your own Meta API key. The address or the key never leaves your device.
